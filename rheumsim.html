About RheumSim

Educational case simulation based on the RheumDx knowledge architecture and progressive case-learning model. It is designed for trainees and clinicians to practise reasoning rather than provide live patient diagnosis.

Important: educational use only. Treatment answers are principles, not patient-specific prescribing instructions. Local guidance, consultant review and current product information remain essential.
