# Clinical safety notes

- Qualified-clinician use only.
- OCR can alter negation, drug names, dates, units, antibody names and numerical results. OCR-derived text must be checked against the source images before analysis.
- The application performs de-identification immediately before clinical reasoning, but clinicians should avoid entering names, MRNs or unnecessary identifiers.
- Classification criteria support research-style classification and structured reasoning; they do not establish or refute a clinical diagnosis by themselves.
- Missing or unreported information is not equivalent to a negative finding.
- Organ-threatening findings and urgent clinical escalation override routine differential scoring.
- Infection, malignancy, medication effects and non-inflammatory mimics must be considered where appropriate.
- This prototype has not undergone prospective clinical validation and should not be used as an autonomous medical device.
